# Warehouse Club Tire Buying (Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's) for a 2013 Kia Sorento, Ancram NY 12502, November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method note: direct page fetches of costco.com, tires.costco.com, tires.bjs.com and samsclub.com (scene7) were blocked in this research environment (DNS blocked by session network policy), so everything below comes from search-engine snippets of official pages plus third-party guides. Prices drawn from cached Sam's Club product-page snapshots may not reflect today's price. **No live Costco or BJ's per-tire price for the Sorento sizes could be verified.** Re-check on tires.costco.com, samsclub.com and tires.bjs.com (enter the vehicle or ZIP 12502) before buying.

## Costco: brands, winter tires, Sorento-size prices, installation package, November 2026 promotions, membership

### Takeaway
Costco US sells Michelin, BFGoodrich, Bridgestone, Firestone and Pirelli. It does not sell Goodyear or Continental, and there is no Kirkland tire. Installation is effectively bundled in the tire price, and the package includes nitrogen, a 5-year road hazard warranty, and lifetime rotation, balancing and flat repair. Costco's official Holiday Savings event runs **Nov 16–30, 2026** with a tire offer on any set of 4 Michelin tires; third-party sites put it at $80 off. I could not verify Costco per-tire prices for 235/65R17, 235/60R18 or 235/55R19, because the Costco sites were inaccessible.

### Cited Findings
**Brands**
- Costco's US Tires & Auto page lists these brands: Bridgestone, Firestone, Michelin, BF Goodrich, Pirelli (plus Velox and Interstate batteries) — [Costco Tires & Auto (via search snippet)](https://www.costco.com/auto-tires.html); [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2131987/costco-tire-brands-for-daily-driver)
- A 2026 guide says Goodyear, Continental and Yokohama are not sold at Costco US. Some low-quality blogs claim Costco sells Goodyear, Continental and a "Kirkland Signature" tire, but I found no evidence of a Kirkland tire line in the US — [Costco Guides – tire brands 2026](https://costcoguides.com/tire-brands-at-costco/); conflicting claim from [thetirereviews.com](https://thetirereviews.com/what-tire-brands-does-costco-carry)

**Winter tires**
- Third-party guides say Costco carries Michelin X-Ice Snow, select sizes of Pirelli Scorpion Winter, and Bridgestone Blizzak (WS90 cited). Pirelli Ice Friction shows only on Costco Canada — [Costco Guides – tire brands](https://costcoguides.com/tire-brands-at-costco/); [tireadvise.com](https://tireadvise.com/what-tire-brands-does-costco-carry/)
- A separate search found no US Costco product listing for Blizzak or X-Ice Snow. Blizzak (including DM-V2) is clearly listed on Costco Canada and Costco UK pages — [tires.costco.ca Bridgestone](https://tires.costco.ca/Bridgestone_Tires); [Costco UK Blizzak](https://www.costco.co.uk/c/Bridgestone-22555-R18-98T-BLIZZAK/tire/p/618914)

**Installation package**
- Costco's own tire site says the tire purchase includes installation at "No charge". A June 2026 source reports the installation line as $0 on eligible purchases — [tires.costco.com (snippet)](https://tires.costco.com/Home?whs=1152); [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-costco-tire-installation-cost/); [Clark.com](https://clark.com/cars/need-new-tires-these-are-10-best-tire-retailers/)
- Third-party sources conflict on the fee: $24.99/tire ($99.96 per set), $18.99/tire plus a $1 disposal fee, and $19.99/tire. These likely reflect older pricing or how the fee is shown on the receipt — [TireAdvise – installation cost 2026](https://tireadvise.com/what-does-costco-tire-installation-cost/); [Costco Guides – prices](https://costcoguides.com/costco-tires-prices/)
- Included: mounting, balancing, new rubber valve stems, nitrogen inflation, lifetime rotation, lifetime balancing, lifetime flat repair, and a road hazard warranty — [TireAdvise](https://tireadvise.com/what-does-costco-tire-installation-cost/); [Jalopnik – free extras](https://www.jalopnik.com/2066433/free-extras-costco-tires/); [Costco Guides – valve stems/TPMS](https://costcoguides.com/costco-tpms-and-valve-stem-cost/)
- Road hazard warranty: 5 years (60 months) from purchase or until tread reaches 2/32", whichever comes first. Credit is prorated for unrepairable damage — [Costco Advantage page (snippet)](https://tires.costco.com/CostcoAdvantage?Length=6); [Fool.com](https://fool.com/the-ascent/personal-finance/articles/5-perks-of-buying-tires-at-costco/)
- Costco installs only tires bought from Costco Tire Center. Tires ordered online ship free to the chosen Costco Tire Center — [tires.costco.com (snippet)](https://tires.costco.com/Home)

**November 2026 promotions**
- Costco's official "Holiday Savings, Valid 11/16/26 - 11/30/26" page includes a tire offer on any set of four Michelin tires (Costco closed Thu Nov 26, 2026). The $80 amount comes from third-party coupon sites, not the snippet of Costco's page — [Costco Holiday Savings page](https://www.costco.com/o/-/pre-thanksgiving-day); [Costco Guides – coupon book Nov 16–Dec 20, 2026](https://costcoguides.com/costco-coupon-book/)
- Conflict: Costcoguides.com says the coupon book for the same window also gives $80 off 4 Bridgestone tires. This is the only source for that claim, and the page is internally inconsistent — [Costco Guides](https://costcoguides.com/costco-coupon-book/)
- The Oct 19–Nov 15, 2026 coupon book has ended — [costrefund.com](https://costrefund.com/costco-coupon-book)
- Recent US Michelin offers were $70–$80 off a set of 4, including one valid through Sep 20, 2026 with a limit of 3 per household. Costco runs Michelin and Bridgestone set-of-4 offers several times a year — [DealNews](https://www.dealnews.com/Michelin-Tire-Deals-at-Costco-80-off-for-members/22192323.html); [Slickdeals daily](https://daily.slickdeals.net/shopping/costco-michelin-tire-discount-promotion); [SlashGear – $80 Michelin until May 10](https://www.slashgear.com/2165328/costco-deal-save-80-dollars-on-set-of-four-michelin-tires/)

**Membership**
- Gold Star costs $65/yr and Executive $130/yr (Executive earns a 2% reward up to $1,250/yr). A price tracker confirmed these on Nov 8, 2026 — [recurdash](https://recurdash.com/subscription-pricing/costco); [ShopBack](https://www.shopback.com/blog/savings/costco-membership-worth)
- Non-members pay 5% more on items they can buy on costco.com. I found no tire-specific rule — [ShopBack](https://www.shopback.com/blog/savings/costco-membership-worth)

**Consumer pros and cons**
- Wait times are the dominant complaint: 1.5–3 hours even with appointments, and some reports of 6–8 hours in winter changeover season. One r/Costco thread is titled "If you value your time don't buy tires from Costco" — [ConsumerAffairs Costco Tires reviews](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/tires/costco.html); [Cheapism](https://www.cheapism.com/costco-tire-center-experience); [r/Costco mirror](https://lr.eu.psf.lt/r/Costco/comments/1gyd1n3/if_you_value_your_time_dont_buy_tires_from_costco)
- Jalopnik advises calling the local warehouse before buying online, because in-store and online prices can differ — [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/2154061/call-costco-tires-cheaper-online-or-in-store/)

### Inferences
- With installation bundled and the Nov 16–30 Michelin offer active, a Michelin set (for example CrossClimate2 or Defender LTX M/S2) bought at Costco in the last two weeks of November is likely the best-value Costco purchase. Buying before Nov 30 matters, because the next set-of-4 offer could be Bridgestone instead.
- Costco shows in-store pricing only to members (you must be a member to get tires installed at the Tire Center). A buyer who isn't already a member should add $65 (Gold Star) to the first-year cost.
- Rough per-tire benchmark for Costco Michelin CrossClimate2 or Defender in these sizes: ~$220–$290 before discount. This is an ESTIMATE based on Sam's Club's comparable listings ($259.99–$265.99, below), not a verified Costco price.

### Gaps
- Costco per-tire prices for 235/65R17, 235/60R18 and 235/55R19 (CrossClimate2, Defender LTX M/S2, Premier LTX, Blizzak DM-V2/DM-V3, X-Ice Snow SUV) could not be retrieved. tires.costco.com and costco.com were blocked by this session's network policy.
- I could not confirm whether Costco US stocks Blizzak DM-V2/DM-V3 or X-Ice Snow SUV in the Sorento sizes this season. Sources conflict.
- The exact dollar amount of the Nov 16–30 Michelin offer ($80 per third-party sites) and whether a Bridgestone offer runs in parallel are unconfirmed from Costco directly.
- I found no source on whether Costco's road hazard warranty is restricted in NY (as Sam's Club's is). This matters little if the buyer uses a CT store.

## Sam's Club: brands, winter tires, install package, road hazard (NY exclusion), promotions, prices

### Takeaway
Sam's Club has the broadest brand selection of the three: Michelin, Bridgestone, Firestone, BFGoodrich, Goodyear and Pirelli. It also sells dedicated winter tires in the Sorento sizes, including Blizzak DM-V2 235/65R17 and Pirelli Scorpion Winter. The Premium Tire Installation Package costs about $20/tire. **Road hazard protection is NOT available on new tire purchases in New York after 3/1/2025**, which matters for the Kingston, Fishkill and Latham clubs. Product pages show "Save $80 + Free Installation for Plus" on Michelin sets ending Dec 31 (year not shown).

### Cited Findings
- The Premium Tire Installation Package is about $20/tire (~$80 per set). It includes mounting, balancing, valve stem service, lifetime rotation, flat repair, TPMS service and Road Hazard Protection — [ThePricer – Sam's install](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-sams-club-tire-installation-cost/); [Sam's Club Tire Installation Package page](https://www.samsclub.com/ip/tire-installation-package/13660205659)

### Inferences
- Example out-the-door ESTIMATE at Sam's Club Kingston for 4 × CrossClimate2 235/60R18 at the snapshot price: $1,063.96 − $80 = $983.96, plus $80 installation ($0 for Plus members, if the promo is live), plus NY sales tax (~8%, my assumption for Ulster County) and NY's waste-tire fee (believed to be $2.50/tire, unverified). That gives roughly $1,080 (Plus) to $1,160 (Club), with no road hazard coverage because the tires are bought in NY.
- For a NY buyer, the missing road hazard coverage at NY Sam's Clubs weakens Sam's value compared with Costco and BJ's, which still advertise road hazard. I found no NY exclusion for Costco or BJ's, but didn't confirm either way.
- Sam's is the strongest warehouse option for a dedicated winter tire in 235/65R17 (Blizzak DM-V2 or Scorpion Winter) because those products are listed online.

### Gaps
- No live November 2026 Sam's Club tire promotion could be confirmed. The "ends Dec 31" promos come from undated snapshots.
- I found no Sam's Club prices for Blizzak DM-V2 235/65R17 or any 235/55R19 tire.
- Sam's membership fees (Club / Plus) for 2026 were not researched in this pass (not found in results).

## BJ's Wholesale: brands, install fee, road hazard, promotions

### Takeaway
BJ's installation is about $20/tire ($80 per set). It includes a road hazard warranty (36 months / 36,000 miles / 2/32") plus rotation, rebalancing, flat repair and inflation checks. BJ's runs frequent brand promos: Michelin "$150–$160 off tires & installation", BFGoodrich $50 off plus $50 off installation (Aug 2026), Cooper and Kelly buy-3-get-1. I could not verify the November 2026 promo because tires.bjs.com was blocked.

### Cited Findings
- Installation is $20/tire ($80 per set), posted as of July 2026. BJ's refunds $20/tire if a center can't install BJ's-purchased tires, and installs only tires bought at BJ's — [ThePricer – BJ's install](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-bjs-tire-installation-cost/); [BJ's help/club info via search](https://help.bjs.com/hc/en-us/articles/26708443022996-Does-my-local-club-have-a-Tire-Center)
- Included services: tire rotation, wheel rebalancing, flat repairs, inflation checks, and a full road hazard warranty. Road hazard runs 36 months or 36,000 miles, or until 2/32" tread, whichever comes first — [BJ's blog – Tire Center FAQ](https://bjs.com/blog/tire-care-101-bj-s-tire-center-s-frequently-asked-questions); [Clark.com – BJ's tires](https://clark.com/save-money/bjs-tires/amp/)
- Promotions seen on tires.bjs.com and bjs.com snippets (mostly undated): "Save $150 Instantly on a set of MICHELIN Tires"; Michelin "save $160 on tires & installation"; BFGoodrich "$50 off a set of 4 + $50 off installation" (Aug 2026); Cooper Endeavor buy 3 get 1 free (+ free installation); Kelly buy 3 get 1 free; Uniroyal 42% off Oct 7–30. Promos vary by club, and some require installation — [tires.bjs.com](https://tires.bjs.com/); [tires.bjs.com/offers](https://tires.bjs.com/offers/); [BJ's BFG product page](https://www.bjs.com/product/bfg-commercial-23585r16-120q/3000000000000724524); [Slickdeals Uniroyal](https://slickdeals.net/f/20121516-bjs-wholesale-club-42-off-all-uniroyal-tires-10-7-to-10-30)
- Brands seen in BJ's promos: Michelin, BFGoodrich, Cooper, Kelly, Uniroyal, Goodyear family (DuraTrac) — [tires.bjs.com](https://tires.bjs.com/); [BJ's DuraTrac page](https://www.bjs.com/product/lt29565r18-e-127124q-wrl-duratrac-rt/3000000000004771309)

### Inferences
- With a $150–$160 Michelin promo active, BJ's can beat Costco's ~$80 Michelin offer, even after paying $80 for installation, if base tire prices are similar. Whether a Michelin promo is live in late November 2026 is unverified.
- BJ's road hazard (3 yr / 36k mi) is shorter than Costco's (5 yr), but unlike NY Sam's Clubs it does appear to be offered.

### Gaps
- No BJ's per-tire prices for the Sorento sizes were retrieved, and no confirmation of BJ's winter tire stock (Blizzak / X-Ice) in those sizes.
- The current (Nov 2026) BJ's promotion is unverified. tires.bjs.com was blocked.
- I could not confirm whether BJ's road hazard has a New York exclusion like Sam's.
- BJ's membership fees for 2026 were not found.

- Returning to a distant Costco for free rotations or flat repair is a recurring cost. Costco honors service at any Costco tire center, but all candidates are 50+ mi away. Sam's Kingston is the most convenient for ongoing service, but has no road hazard in NY.
