Research date: 2026-11-17. Method note: WebFetch was blocked in this session for tyrereviews.com, tire-reviews.com, tirerack.com and qz.com (DNS not on the session's allowed network list). ## Q1. Best dedicated winter tires in recent (2024-2026) independent tests and surveys, by tier

### Takeaway
The 2026-27 premium winter lineup has changed. Bridgestone's new Blizzak IcePeak replaces the WS90 and DM-V2 and won Tire Rack's 2026 new-winter-tire test. Michelin's X-Ice Snow+ replaces both the X-Ice Snow and the X-Ice Snow SUV. Consumer Reports' 2026 top SUV winter pick is the Nokian Hakkapeliitta R5 SUV. Tire Rack's 2026 guide names the Continental VikingContact 8 best winter tire for SUVs/crossovers. In the budget tier, CR named the GT Radial IcePro SUV 3 (~$103-112) as the backup to the Nokian with "similar scores overall". That makes it the standout value pick, with General Altimax Arctic 12 and Firestone Winterforce 2 UV (~$153-162) as the other well-known budget choices.

## Q2. Best-value all-weather (3PMSF) tires, and how they compare with dedicated winter tires on ice and deep snow

### Takeaway
Michelin CrossClimate2 is the consistent leader. It won Tire Rack's 2026 all-weather-vs-winter test overall, topped CR's all-season SUV ratings, and is Tire Rack's best all-weather pick for 2026. It is also the most expensive (~$250-268 for 235/65R17). Goodyear Assurance WeatherReady 2 (~$240-251) is the strongest challenger. Firestone WeatherGrip (~$192) and Falken Aklimate (~$168) are the value tier. On snow and especially ice, even the best all-weather tire clearly trails dedicated winter tires: the five winter tires took the top five snow spots in the 2026 Tire Rack test.

## Q3. One all-weather set year-round vs a dedicated winter set (plus summer/all-season), including the steel-wheel argument

### Takeaway
For a 2013 Sorento in rural, hilly Ancram (cold winters, ice, and possibly unplowed roads), a budget-to-mid winter set on cheap wheels gives the most winter safety per dollar. Pairing GT Radial IcePro SUV 3 (~$110) with existing all-seasons gives a 4-tire winter set for roughly $440-470 plus wheels. One CrossClimate2 set costs about $1,000+. A single all-weather set is the simpler, cheaper-overall option if the car is near end-of-life or does few winter miles, but it gives up meaningful ice and deep-snow capability.

### Cited Findings
- Winter tires took the top five snow places, and the WS90 led ice braking and acceleration, in the 2026 Tire Rack all-weather-vs-winter test — [tyrereviews summary](https://www.tyrereviews.com/Tyre-Tests/2026-tire-rack-all-weather-vs-winter-tyre-test.htm)
- Set-of-4 prices for comparison: IcePeak $743.96 ($663.96 after rebate) — [Big O](https://www.bigotires.com/tires/sku/bridgestone/blizzak-icepeak/235-65r17-108t-xl/000000000001163834), [Tire Rack](https://www.tirerack.com/tires/tires.jsp?tireMake=Bridgestone&tireModel=Blizzak+IcePeak&partnum=365TR7BZIPXL); CrossClimate2 CUV ~$1,000 at $249.99 each — [1010tires](https://www.1010tires.com/Tires/235-65R17/All+Weather); IcePro SUV 3 ~$446 at $111.54 each — [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/GT-Radial-IcePro-SUV-3-Winter-235-65R17-108T-XL-SUV-Crossover-Tire/567104904)
- Wheels: the 2013 Sorento uses 5x114.3, 17x7 ET48, 67.1 mm hub bore — [tirewheelguide](https://tirewheelguide.com/sizes/kia/sorento/2013/), [threepiece.us](https://vehicles.threepiece.us/vehicles/kia/sorento/2013/ex-fwd/). A set of four replacement OEM-style 17" alloys costs $763.52 — [reach.dog](https://reach.dog/shop/factory-wheel-replacement/products/set-of-4-new-17-2011-2013-kia-sorento-silver-replacement-alloy-wheels). No steel-wheel price was found.

### Inferences
- Cost logic (estimates, not cited prices):
  - A second set of wheels avoids seasonal mount/balance fees on each swap. Typical shop mount/balance is about $20-35 per tire per changeover (background knowledge, unverified). Over 3-4 seasons that roughly pays for steel wheels, which typically run ~$60-100 each in 17" (estimate; not found in sources). Running two sets also halves wear on each.
  - A 2013 vehicle's remaining life matters. If the owner plans to keep the car 3+ winters, winter tires on wheels pay off. If they plan to sell within 1-2 years, one all-weather set (Falken Aklimate ~$168 or WeatherGrip ~$192) is the cheaper path.
- Drivers on 18" or 19" OEM wheels could save money by downsizing the winter set to 17" (235/65R17). Winter tire prices in that size are lower and the taller sidewall suits potholed rural roads. This needs brake-clearance confirmation, but the stock 17" wheel fits the 2013 model per tirewheelguide.
- Dedicated winter tires should not be run year-round: the soft compound wears fast in summer heat. That is general knowledge, not a cited finding.

### Gaps
- No source was found for steel-wheel package pricing for a 2013 Sorento, or for local Hudson Valley (Columbia County) mount/balance or tire-swap prices.
- No source was found with a quantified Ancram/Columbia County snowfall or ice-day climate. That is outside this note's scope; another researcher may cover it.
