# Cheapest Ways to Buy 4 Tires (Online vs. National Chains) for a 2013 Kia Sorento, Ancram NY 12502 (November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method note: direct page fetches of tirerack.com and tirecoupons.com were blocked from this research environment (host not reachable), so all retailer prices below come from search-engine result snippets/summaries of the cited pages. Snippet capture dates are often unknown; treat every price as "seen in mid-November 2026 search results, verify at checkout." Totals marked "ESTIMATE" are my arithmetic, not quoted prices.

## 0. Vehicle fitment (context for all prices)

### Takeaway
2013 Sorento came on three OE sizes depending on trim: 235/65R17 (LX), 235/60R18 (LX/EX/SX), 235/55R19 (SX/Limited). Check the door-jamb placard before ordering; 17" is by far the cheapest size to buy for.

### Cited Findings
- OE size P235/65R17 103T for LX; P235/60R18 102H for EX/LX/SX — [SizeMyTires](https://www.sizemytires.com/vehicle/2013/kia/sorento)
- 235/55R19 101V on 7.5Jx19 wheels listed as 2013 factory fitment; 235/60R18 on 7Jx18 also listed — [Wheel-Size.com](https://www.wheel-size.com:443/size/kia/sorento/2013/)
- 19" size shown on SX / SX Limited (2014 page; 2013 likely same) — [iSeeCars 2014](https://www.iseecars.com/car/2014-kia-sorento-tire-size)

### Inferences
- A 19" owner can drop to 17" or 18" for a winter set (wheel-and-tire package) and save significantly on the tires themselves; Tire Rack's packages for adjacent years default to 17".

### Gaps
- No 2013-specific source mapped 235/55R19 to a named trim.

## 5. New York State fees and Columbia County sales tax

### Takeaway
NY charges a $2.50/new-tire waste tire management and recycling fee (extended through Dec 31, 2027), and since Sept 1, 2025 installers must collect it on tires they didn't sell unless the buyer proves it was paid — so online buyers pay it too. Columbia County combined sales tax is 8% (4% state + 4% county).

### Cited Findings
- Waste tire management and recycling fee: $2.50 per new tire on retail sales delivered in NY; excludes used/recapped, mail-order, and resale; extended through Dec 31, 2027 — [NYS Tax Dept](https://tax.ny.gov/bus/tire/wtm.htm)
- Effective Sept 1, 2025, a tire service installing tires it did not sell must collect the $2.50 fee per new tire unless the purchaser shows it was previously paid — [NYS Tax Dept](https://tax.ny.gov/bus/tire/wtm.htm)
- Seller retains $0.25 of each $2.50 — [NYS Tax memo](https://tax.ny.gov/pdf/memos/misc/m16_4m.pdf)
- Statute requires separately stated fee (or "included in price" notice) — [ECL 27-1905](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ENV/27-1905); [DEC sample sign](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/docs/materials_minerals_pdf/tiresign.pdf)
- Columbia County combined sales tax 8% (no additional local tax; Hudson also 8%) — [SalesTaxHandbook](https://www.salestaxhandbook.com/new-york/rates/columbia-county); [Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/taxrates/state-rates/new-york/counties/columbia.html)
- A 2023 bill extended Columbia County's authority for an additional 1% (part of the 4% county rate) — [NY Senate A7098](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2023/A7098)

### Inferences
- For 4 tires: waste-tire fee = $10.00. Sales tax at 8% applies to tires and, in NY, typically to installation labor too (tire installation is generally a taxable service in NY — not verified in this session). Massachusetts purchases (Great Barrington) would be taxed at MA's 6.25% and not subject to the NY $2.50 fee if bought and installed in MA, though savings (~1.75% + $10) are small vs. driving cost.
- Online retailers shipping to NY addresses will generally collect NY sales tax (economic nexus) — not verified per retailer.

### Gaps
- Did not confirm via NYS Tax publication that tire installation labor is taxable; did not verify each online retailer's NY tax collection.
